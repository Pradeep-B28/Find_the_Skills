# Reel Scripts for Job Post X-Ray

These four short-form video scripts are timed directly to the automated demo mode (`?demo=1`).
Note: All sample job descriptions used in these scripts are synthetic examples written by Wrench Wise for educational demonstration. They do not represent any real company or actual job posting.

---

## Script 1: Full Stack Developer (Vertical, 30 Seconds)

- **URL to Record**: `http://localhost:8085/?demo=1&sample=s1`
- **Aspect Ratio**: 9:16 (Vertical)
- **Duration**: 30 Seconds

### Timeline and Visual Shot List

- **00:00 - 00:02 (0s to 2s)**
  - **On-Screen Text (Hook)**: Job posts look scary. Let's X-ray one.
  - **Visual**: Home screen. The tap dot focuses on the text box and pastes a dense Full Stack Developer job description.
  - **Voiceover**: Job postings look like an endless wall of requirements. Let us run an X-ray on one.
  - **Caption**: Job postings look like an endless wall of text.

- **00:02 - 00:08 (2s to 8s)**
  - **Visual**: Tap dot clicks "X-ray this job post". The scan screen opens. The green beam glides down the text strip, highlighting React, Node.js, TypeScript, Docker, and PostgreSQL. Counter climbs to 19 skills.
  - **Voiceover**: Watch the scanner strip away the fluff. It instantly pulls out the exact languages, frameworks, and databases required.
  - **Caption**: In seconds, every core skill and tool is separated from the noise.

- **00:08 - 00:16 (8s to 16s)**
  - **Visual**: Tap dot clicks "Tick the skills you have". The checklist opens. Tap dot selects JavaScript, React, HTML, CSS, Git, and SQL one by one.
  - **Voiceover**: You do not need to know everything on day one. Tick the skills you already know.
  - **Caption**: Tick what you already know. You usually have more than you think.

- **00:16 - 00:26 (16s to 26s)**
  - **Visual**: Tap dot clicks "Show my match". The result screen loads. The circular match ring animates to 37 percent. It scrolls down to the Full Stack Web Development cohort card showing 10 of 12 missing skills covered.
  - **Voiceover**: Here is your real score. You have 7 skills, and you need 12 more. The program card shows the fastest path to bridge the gap.
  - **Caption**: See your true match percentage and the exact tools you need next.

- **00:26 - 00:30 (26s to 30s)**
  - **Visual**: Screen rests on the Wrench Wise WhatsApp button and curriculum overview.
  - **Voiceover**: Try it free on any job post at Wrench Wise.
  - **Caption**: Free tool at job-post-xray.vercel.app by Wrench Wise.
